## Find your way around

- **Guides** take you through a task and explain how to check the result.
- **Settings** describe individual options and where to find them.
- **Skins, add-ons and appendices** extend the handbook and share its search.

The button labels at the bottom of the screen tell you which actions are available. Appearance depends on the skin, so follow the labels rather than screen positions.

<p class="intro-note">This first edition follows OpenATV 8.0 and covers the shared interface. Special cases will be added in the appendices.</p>
