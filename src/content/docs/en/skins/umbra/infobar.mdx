| Cover | Portrait programme artwork on the left. |
| Panorama | Wide preview/backdrop on the left. |

Without usable e2MDB data, the base presentation or intended fallback remains. Cover and Panorama are artwork, not a second live-TV window. A screenshot's video dimensions alone do not establish the OSD resolution.

The layout of this full Infobar is separate from OpenATV's **Lite Infobar** and its **second INFO/ECM page**. Those controls, including MENU → MENU, are explained under [additional menus and Infobar views](../menues-infoleisten/). They do not require changing the Classic/Cover/Panorama preference.

For artwork, first configure [e2MDB](../../../addons/e2mdb/). Changing a skin layout does not fill the database or turn on all metadata integration switches.
