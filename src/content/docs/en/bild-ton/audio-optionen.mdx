These are all **24 fields** in Audio Settings at the checked source revision. Visibility and choices depend on the driver. Each receiver shows only its supported subset.

**MENU → Setup → Audio → Audio Settings**. [Setup and context](../audio-grundlagen/). Native names and configuration keys help locate a setting. Source defaults can differ from saved values or image presets.

## Volume steps

1–10 steps for a short volume press; baseline 1. No sound processing.

Key: `config.volumeControl.pressStep`.

## Long press volume steps

1–10 steps while holding volume; baseline 5. Larger values change level faster.

Key: `config.volumeControl.longStep`.

## Volume/Mute display timer

1–10 seconds for volume/mute indicators, baseline 3. Does not change actual muting.

Key: `config.volumeControl.hideTimer`.

## PCM Multichannel

Yes/No. Allows decoded multichannel PCM on supported driver/HDMI paths. Other downmix settings can affect availability; PCM is not necessarily stereo.

Key: `config.av.pcm_multichannel`.

## AC3 downmix

Yes normally mixes Dolby Digital to stereo; No forwards AC3 on a supported path. Check the AVR decoder/connection first. Source baseline: Yes.

Key: `config.av.downmix_ac3`.

## Passthrough audio handling delay AC3

Platform-specific AC3 switching wait, baseline 100 ms. Adjust for relevant zap-related audio problems, not general lip sync.

Key: `config.av.passthrough_fix_short`.

## Passthrough audio handling delay AC3+

Longer wait for AC3+/Atmos format changes, baseline 1200 ms. A larger value can delay audio onset.

Key: `config.av.passthrough_fix_long`.

## AC3 plus transcoding

Platform choices can include HDMI-controlled/best, passthrough, downmix, AC3, multichannel PCM, DTS or forced AC3+. Use only offered supported paths. AC3 conversion does not generally preserve Atmos.

Key: `config.av.transcodeac3plus`.

## AC4

Where available: best/HDMI-controlled, passthrough or downmix. AC4 is not synonymous with Atmos; source, decoder and destination must match.

Key: `config.av.ac4`.

## DTS downmix

Yes for DTS stereo decoding, No for supported DTS passthrough. Treat DTS-HD variants separately.

Key: `config.av.downmix_dts`.

## DTS/DTS-HD HR/DTS-HD MA/DTS:X

Depending on platform: downmix, DTS conversion, multichannel PCM, passthrough or HDMI-controlled selection. DTS conversion is not lossless DTS-HD output.

Key: `config.av.dtshd`.

## Dolby TrueHD

Where offered: downmix, passthrough or best HDMI output. Lossless source audio and Atmos extensions require a suitable complete playback path.

Key: `config.av.truehd`.

## WMA Pro

Driver-dependent downmix, passthrough, multichannel PCM or best HDMI output. The selector does not itself provide a universal WMA Pro decoder.

Key: `config.av.wmapro`.

## AAC downmix

Depending on platform: Yes/No or choices including downmix, passthrough, multichannel PCM, HDMI-controlled and AC3/DTS conversion. TV/AVR support for AAC bitstream is not guaranteed.

Key: `config.av.downmix_aac`.

## AAC plus downmix

Select the offered downmix/passthrough mode for AAC+/HE-AAC. It is different from Dolby Digital Plus.

Key: `config.av.downmix_aacplus`.

## AAC transcoding

Separate AAC encoding path with driver-defined choices, such as off, AC3 or DTS when offered. Avoid conflicting downmix rules.

Key: `config.av.transcodeaac`.

## Audio Source

Driver-internal HDMI audio source, such as PCM/S/PDIF or Bluetooth on some platforms. Not ARC and not necessarily selection of a physical output cable.

Key: `config.av.audio_source`.

## General AC3 delay

−1000 to +1000 ms in 5 ms steps, baseline 0. Positive values delay the corresponding Dolby/compressed audio path. Verify per-player behaviour.

Key: `config.av.generalAC3delay`.

## General PCM delay

Same range for decoded PCM. Adjust one place at a time when several devices have delay controls.

Key: `config.av.generalPCMdelay`.

## 3D Surround

Off or processing on HDMI, S/PDIF or DAC where offered. Virtual surround does not add discrete source channels.

Key: `config.av.surround_3d`.

## 3D Surround Speaker Position

Centre, wide or extra wide: virtual speaker placement for active 3D Surround. Not physical AVR speaker calibration.

Key: `config.av.surround_3d_speaker`.

## Audio Auto Volume Level

Off or HDMI/S/PDIF/DAC, driver-dependent. Audio-path level processing, separate from per-service offsets. Unchanged bitstreams may be unaffected.

Key: `config.av.autovolume`.

## Enable BT audio

On/Off for a supported Bluetooth audio path. Pairing and hardware setup are separate; not available on every receiver.

Key: `config.av.btaudio`.

## General BT audio delay

Driver-dependent Bluetooth delay: −1000 to +1000 ms in 5 ms steps, baseline 0. Check actual latency with the connected device.

Key: `config.av.btaudiodelay`.
