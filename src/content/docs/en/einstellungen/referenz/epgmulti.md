This reference contains the help text provided by openATV. The open dialog and selected options determine which entries are visible.

## Where do I find it?

**Main Menu → Setup → EPG → MultiEPG Settings**

Search also matches technical keys. [Back to the directory](../../)

<h2 id="option-b61aeabd396e">Channel preview mode</h2>

<p>Select &#x27;Yes&#x27; to enable preview of channels while in the EPG list.</p>

<details>
<summary>Reference & notes</summary>

`config.epgselection.multi_preview_mode`

Setup level: Expert.

</details>

<h2 id="option-34a0127e0a9f">Show bouquet on launch</h2>

<p>Select &#x27;Yes&#x27; to show the bouquet selection screen every time you open the EPG.</p>

<details>
<summary>Reference & notes</summary>

`config.epgselection.multi_showbouquet`

Setup level: Expert.

</details>

<h2 id="option-33e891925f03">Skip empty services</h2>

<p>Select &#x27;Yes&#x27; to hide channels without EPG data.</p>

<details>
<summary>Reference & notes</summary>

Setup level: Expert.

</details>
