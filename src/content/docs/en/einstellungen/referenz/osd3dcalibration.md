This reference contains the help text provided by openATV. The open dialog and selected options determine which entries are visible.

## Where do I find it?

**Main Menu → Setup → Video → OSD 3D Settings**

Search also matches technical keys. [Back to the directory](../../)

<h2 id="option-c40deb78a69a">3D Mode</h2>

<p>This option lets you choose the 3D mode of the OSD.</p>

<details>
<summary>Reference & notes</summary>

`config.osd.threeDmode`

Setup level: Simple.

</details>

<h2 id="option-a72f309c43b9">Depth</h2>

<p>This option lets you adjust the 3D depth of the OSD.</p>

<details>
<summary>Reference & notes</summary>

`config.osd.threeDznorm`

Setup level: Simple.

</details>

<h2 id="option-f6db7c981137">Show in Extension Menu</h2>

<p>This option lets you show the 3D OSD menu option in the Extension Menu screen.</p>

<details>
<summary>Reference & notes</summary>

`config.osd.show3dextensions`

Setup level: Simple.

</details>
