This reference contains the help text provided by OpenATV. The open dialog and selected options determine which entries are visible.

## Where do I find it?

The direct menu location has not yet been mapped in this first edition. Look for **Hard Disk Settings**.

Search also matches technical keys. [Back to the directory](../../)

<h2 id="option-5f2c3cb88eaa">Hard disk Standby after</h2>

<p>Configure the hard disk drive to go to Standby after the specified idle time.</p>

<details>
<summary>Reference & notes</summary>

`config.usage.hdd_standby`

Setup level: Simple.

</details>

<h2 id="option-c3663875d809">Hard disk Standby in receiver Standby after</h2>

<p>Configure the hard disk drive to go to Standby after the specified idle time when the receiver is in Standby.</p>

<details>
<summary>Reference & notes</summary>

`config.usage.hdd_standby_in_standby`

Setup level: Simple.

</details>

<h2 id="option-22ced303777b">Use Harddisk Hardware Standby Timer</h2>

<p>Option to enable the hardware Standby timer.</p>

<details>
<summary>Reference & notes</summary>

`config.usage.hdd_timer`

Setup level: Simple.

</details>

<h2 id="option-d7db49f4fb29">Show unknown devices</h2>

<p>Enable this option to show unknown storage devices in device manager.</p>

<details>
<summary>Reference & notes</summary>

`config.usage.showUnknownDevices`

Setup level: Expert.

</details>
