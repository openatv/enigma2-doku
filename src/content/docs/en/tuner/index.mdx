**Start with the cable and the distribution system.** Knowing what the socket supplies is the basis for configuring the tuner and the scan. Installing a channel list does not configure the reception path.

## From connection to first picture

1. [Check the wiring drawings](./verkabelung/).
2. Configure the connection: [single satellite feed](./konfiguration/), [cable TV](./dvb-c/) or [DVB-T2 aerial](./dvb-t2/).
3. For shared systems, establish the [Unicable allocation](./unicable/) or [DiSEqC satellite mapping](./diseqc/) first.
4. [Choose a scan method](./suchlauf/) and [check reception](./signaldiagnose/).
5. Understand [recording and watching with one tuner](./single-tuner/) before configuring additional tuners.

## Where are the controls?

Start at **Menu → Setup → Tuners, Scanning & Reception**. “Tuner Configuration” configures the connections; “Tuner Settings” contains general tuner selection and operating rules. Plugins extend this menu depending on installation and hardware.
