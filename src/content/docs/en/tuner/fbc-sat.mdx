**Two satellite inputs can supply eight transponders simultaneously. The antenna installation determines which transponders are available.** FBC does not remove the restrictions of a conventional satellite cable. With eight correctly configured Unicable user bands, however, eight independently selectable reception paths become available.

## What does FBC mean?

FBC stands for **Full Band Capture**. A broadly captured input spectrum feeds several **demodulators**. Each demodulator processes one transponder, which may carry several television and radio services. The DVB-S FBC blocks covered here have **eight demodulators and two RF inputs**. FBC as a general technology name does not guarantee this count for every hardware design.

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| FBC block / module | One reception unit sharing input circuitry between several demodulators |
| SAT IN A and SAT IN B | Two physical coaxial sockets; case labels may differ |
| Root tuners A/B | The tuners associated with the two inputs of the satellite block shown here |
| Virtual / internal tuners C–H | Additional real demodulators with internally assigned signal paths; not six more sockets |
| Transponder | A reception package identified by frequency, polarisation and satellite position |
| Channel / service | One individual programme within a transponder |
| User band / SCR | An individual reception frequency in a Unicable system carrying a requested transponder |

The letters are examples. Another tuner order may place the block later in the alphabet. Identify the **block and its two inputs**, rather than relying on letters alone.

## Which connection do I have?

| Connection | Available simultaneously | Setup guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One cable from a universal LNB or conventional multiswitch | Up to eight transponders from **one currently selected satellite plane** | [One universal feed](../fbc-sat-universal/#one-universal-cable) |
| Two independent conventional subscriber feeds | Up to eight transponders from **up to two selected planes** | [Two universal feeds](../fbc-sat-universal/#two-independent-universal-cables) |
| One Unicable cable, eight exclusively assigned user bands | Eight independently selected transponders from the positions and planes provided by the installation | [Unicable step by step](../fbc-sat-unicable/) |
| Two feeds from different fixed satellite positions | Each input's own position and selected plane, eight demodulators in total | [Separate Astra and Hotbird feeds](../fbc-sat-universal/#two-fixed-positions-without-a-diseqc-switch) |
| Fixed dish plus separate motor dish | The fixed position plus the motor dish's current position, still subject to plane restrictions | [Fixed dish and motor](../fbc-sat-universal/#fixed-dish-plus-separate-motor-dish) |

A **single universal LNB** with one output does not become two independent feeds when a splitter is added. A **Unicable LNB**, however, can supply several independent reception paths through its one SCR output. The number of sockets alone therefore does not identify its capabilities.

## “FBC automatic” or “FBC SCR (Unicable/JESS)”?

**FBC automatic** is the normal setting for internal tuners with conventional universal feeds. Enigma2 assigns free demodulators to a usable root tuner as required. It does not discover free user bands or configure eight SCRs.

With **Unicable/JESS**, each independently required demodulator receives its own SCR assignment. Select **FBC SCR (Unicable/JESS)** for C–H; A/B use the appropriate advanced Unicable configuration. An unconfigured internal tuner does not replace an assigned SCR. See the complete [A–H assignment](../fbc-sat-unicable/).

## Watching television and recording simultaneously

When one service is recorded, other services on that transponder can often share its demodulator. A second transponder requires another demodulator and must be reachable through the available feeds. **Eight transponders is neither a fixed maximum of eight channels nor a promise of eight pictures displayed on the television.**

For example, two recordings and live TV from one transponder may share a reception path. Eight recordings from eight different transponders can occupy all eight demodulators. Another recording from an already received transponder may still be possible; a ninth independently selected transponder is not.

Decryption/CI, demux resources, video decoders, storage and network throughput can impose further limits. Fast Channel Change, PiP, streaming, EPG scans and active timers may already occupy reception paths. Greyed-out services do not automatically indicate a faulty tuner.

## Common misunderstandings

| Observation / assumption | Explanation and next check |
| --- | --- |
| “I have eight tuners. Why are some channels unavailable?” | One conventional cable supplies one plane. Check the feed type and planes required simultaneously. |
| “Two inputs mean 16 tuners.” | Both inputs of the block shown here supply eight demodulators in total. |
| “A always supplies four tuners and B supplies four.” | Conventional FBC automatic assigns C–H as required; there is no fixed 4+4 split. |
| “One cable means B must always be disabled.” | The internal feed is what matters: a supported connection lets B share A’s conventional plane. With Unicable, **logical B** can receive internally through A using its own SCR. |
| “Equal to A automatically gives B another SCR.” | Equal configuration and an individual user-band assignment are different things. |
| “Unicable needs eight cables.” | Eight user bands travel together along one suitable coaxial cable. |
| “I must scan eight times.” | The channel list is shared. One complete scan per required reception source is enough. |
| “A strong signal proves that SCR allocation is correct.” | Duplicate SCRs may fail only during concurrent use. Compare both numbers and frequencies. |

## Testing an FBC installation

1. Start with a free-to-air service and check lock and signal quality.
2. Select free-to-air services from **different transponders** for overlapping recordings. Different channel names alone are not sufficient evidence.
3. With universal feeds, stay within the available planes. With eight allocated Unicable UBs, test up to eight different transponders.
4. Check tuner/service information for resource use. Live TV or a stream may already consume a reception path.
5. Play back short sections of the recordings and check picture and sound. Stop/remove the test timers afterwards.
