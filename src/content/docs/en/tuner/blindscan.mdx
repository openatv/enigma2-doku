**Menu → Setup → Tuners, Scanning & Reception → Blind scan.** The plugin discovers carriers without a complete initial frequency list. It requires a supported tuner and matching manufacturer backend. Installing the plugin alone is insufficient.

<Capture id="rx-blindscan" language="en" alt="Blindscan form with search type, frequency range, symbol rates and filters" caption="Native form for a universal Ku connection. The image series shows options without executing a blind scan." />

1. Select a configured tuner and satellite.
2. Choose **Scan for transponders** to inspect carriers first, or **channel scan** to discover services afterwards.
3. Match frequency range, polarisation and symbol-rate range to the installation. Symbol rates here are in **MSym/s**.
4. Check clearing and FTA settings. Filter known transponders, duplicates and adjacent positions according to the intended search.
5. Start with **Green/Scan** and wait for results. In the transponder list, select wanted candidates and start the service scan from them.

**Blue/Restore defaults**, where shown, resets plugin values. **Yellow/Open XML**, where results exist, opens the XML output. **EXIT/Exit** leaves the dialog. Displayed actions can change during a scan.

All **23 extracted form variants** appear in the [Blindscan reference section](../scan-optionen/#blindscan). These include C-band and user-defined inverted-LNB frequency boundaries, device-specific step size and discovered-transponder selection. Copying another receiver’s values cannot add unsupported features.

The [module overview](../scan-module/) explains each filter and XML results under `/tmp`. Check unexpected satellite positions and duplicate carriers before replacing your service database. A full scan cannot correct dish alignment or a wrong DiSEqC port.
